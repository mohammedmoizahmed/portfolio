About Me

A bit more about my background

I got into IT support the way a lot of people do, by being the person friends and coworkers called when something broke. That turned into three years on international service desks supporting over 500 users, eventually becoming a Subject Matter Expert and training new hires. Somewhere along the way, I realized I wasn’t just interested in fixing issues; I wanted to understand why they happened. Why a network drops packets at 2am. Why a single config change ripples through an entire device fleet. That curiosity is what pushed me towards Linux and AWS. I’ve been building my foundation from the ground up, learning the command line, managing users and permissions, deploying EC2 instances, configuring security groups, and understanding how systems behave under real workloads. Every day I’m picking up something new, whether it’s troubleshooting a service on Linux or automating a small deployment on AWS. I’m based in Riyadh, still looking for the next thing to break so I can figure out how it works.
